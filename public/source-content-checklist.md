Across the pathways, the site also includes role-specific audiences, blended method comparisons, accessible and low-bandwidth design notes, practical evaluation evidence, the proposed $7,000 pilot budget, implementation challenges, and links to the referenced workplace and wildfire resources. Program targets are presented as targets, not achieved outcomes. FireReady remains educational awareness and does not replace employer procedures, required training or qualified practical instruction.

The supplied PDFs and locally hosted mining video remain in `public/reference-materials/` and `public/`.
